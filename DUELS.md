# Duel results — Make the AI Judge Laugh (office life, ≤200 chars)

Judge criteria, in order: (1) about office life and lands as a joke,
(2) true to the office, (3) surprising turn / unexpected last word,
(4) tight, every word earns its place, reads cleanly aloud,
(5) original, not a stock line with nouns swapped.

Each pair was judged in both orders (A/B and B/A) to cancel position
bias; the margin is 1 (by a hair) to 5 (overwhelmingly better).
A win counts only when the same draft wins in both orders.
